# AUREN Bug & Maths Bounty Program

> **Program Pool:** $400 USDC  
> **Focus:** Verified engine math bugs, reproducibility, risk integrity, and community contributions.

AUREN is built in public with transparent risk mathematics. We reward developers, quants, and early builders who stress-test our calculations, discover bugs, improve the product, or integrate the AUREN Risk API.

---

## Reward Tiers

| Category | What Qualifies | Payout |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **Maths Bounty** | Finding a financial risk metric calculated incorrectly with mathematical proof and code reproduction. | **$50 USDC** |
| **Bug Bounty** | Reproducible crash, logic error, or data parsing defect reported via GitHub Issues. | **$10–$50 USDC** (by severity) |
| **API Integration** | First 6 developers/agents who call the AUREN Risk API (`/risk`) from an external agent or dashboard and share a demo. | **$25 USDC** each |
| **Shipped Idea** | High-impact product or metric suggestion implemented in a release. | **$50 USDC** + release credit |

## X (Twitter) Community Participation

We believe in building AUREN in public and growing through the developer community.

We encourage every participant to share their contribution and experience with AUREN on X.

**What you can post about:**
- What AUREN is building and its approach to financial risk analysis.
- Your experience testing AUREN's risk engine.
- Mathematical bugs you discovered or calculations you verified.
- Your API integrations, dashboards, or external agent demos.
- New features, improvements, and ideas for AUREN.
- Your experience participating in the AUREN Bounty Program.

**Posting Requirements:**

1. Publish a genuine post about AUREN on X.
2. Mention [@Aurenz_io](https://x.com/Aurenz_io) in your post.
3. Share what you tested, built, discovered, or contributed.
4. Include screenshots, demos, or results where applicable.
5. Submit your X post link along with your GitHub Issue or contribution.

We encourage authentic technical content rather than generic promotional posts.

## Early Builder Badge

All verified testers, bug reporters, and API integrators receive the **AUREN Early Builder Badge** (non-transferable proof of contribution).

Contributors who actively share their work on X also help build AUREN's public development community.

---

## Submission Guidelines

1. Open a GitHub Issue using the appropriate issue tag (`bug`, `idea`, or `question`).
2. Include:
   - Reproduction steps (exact portfolio weights and inputs).
   - Expected vs. actual calculation results.
   - Code snippet or test case reproducing the issue.
   - Screenshots or demo where applicable.
3. Share your contribution on X and mention **@Aurenz_io**.
4. Include your X post link in your submission.
5. Submissions are reviewed within 24 hours.

**Payouts:** Rewards are paid directly in USDC on Base / Ethereum after verification.
